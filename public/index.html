Yes. The media kit, verified stats, rate estimates and sharing tools are free forever. Essential adds extras for $4.99 a month, and you never need it to use CreatorGG.

Do you post anything or see my password?

No. Signing in with Twitch or YouTube only gives us read-only access to your public channel stats. We never post, and we never see your password.

I'm not a gaming creator. Can I use it?

Absolutely. Rates adjust for finance, beauty, tech, lifestyle, fitness and many more niches.

What about TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X?

Sign in with Twitch or YouTube, then add your TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X stats yourself. They're shown on your kit as self-reported, and included in your rate estimates.

Why would brands trust my kit?

Stats from Twitch and YouTube are pulled directly from the platform and marked "Verified", and featured videos show their real numbers. Anything you type in yourself is clearly labeled "self-reported".
