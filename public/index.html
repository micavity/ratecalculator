Tips for pricing your first deals

  • Quote the top of your range first. Brands expect to negotiate.
  • Charge extra for usage rights. If a brand wants to run your content as an ad, that's usually +30–100%.
  • Exclusivity costs more. Agreeing not to work with competitors for a period should add to the price.
  • Bundles sell well. Offer a stream + video + short package at a small discount.
  • Get it in writing. Agree on deliverables, deadlines and payment date before you start.
