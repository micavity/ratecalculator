The details

Who counts as my referral?

Anyone who creates a new CreatorGG account through your link within 30 days of clicking it, or who enters your code at checkout. Existing accounts and your own accounts don't count.

When do I get paid?

Earnings are held for 30 days in case a payment is refunded. Once your available earnings reach $25, we send them to your PayPal email, usually once a month. Add your PayPal email on your dashboard.

What do the people I refer get?

20% off their first 3 months of Essential or Pro. It's applied automatically when they sign up through your link, or they can type your code at checkout.

Do I have to say I earn from it?

Yes. When you share your link or code, make it clear you earn from it, for example with "#ad" or "affiliate link". That's the law in many places, and it keeps your audience's trust.

Is anything not allowed?

Don't refer yourself, create fake accounts, spam people or make misleading claims. Abuse means earnings are cancelled. Full rules are in our Terms of Service.
